# Photo shot list

Real photos of our own fish, taken on a phone. No AI images of fish. Customers should see exactly what they will get.

## Setup for every photo
- **Background:** a plain white tray, white board or white tabletop. Nothing else in the frame (no newspaper, no stall clutter, no logo).
- **Light:** daylight only. Shoot next to an open door or window, or outside in open shade. No flash. No yellow shop bulbs.
- **No shadows:** stand so your body does not block the light. If a hard shadow falls on the fish, move into shade or hold a white sheet on the dark side.
- **Phone:** wipe the lens. Use the normal back camera (1x). Hold the phone flat above the fish for top views. Do not use filters, beauty mode or HDR effects.
- **Fish:** fresh from the ice, wet and shiny, wiped of loose water and blood. Lay it straight. Change the tray cloth or wipe the tray between fish.
- **Frame:** fish fills about two thirds of the frame, centred, with white space around it. The site crops to different shapes, so keep the fish away from the edges.
- **Take 3 to 5 of each, pick the best.**

## Shots per product

Every product gets at least **3 photos**; the product page shows up to 4.

| # | Shot | Angle | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hero | Top view, phone flat above, fish centred | Main image on cards and top of product page |
| 02 | Side or 45 degree | Phone at 45 degrees, fish side on, eye and gills clear | Product page gallery |
| 03 | Close up | Phone 15 to 20 cm away, on the eyes, skin or cut surface | Shows freshness |
| 04 | Scale | Fish (or pack) on a kitchen scale showing the weight | Builds trust on weight |

### By product type

**Whole fresh fish** (pomfret, sardine, mackerel, seer fish whole)
- 01: one fish, or a small group for small fish like sardine, top view on white tray.
- 02: side view, head to the left, eye clear and bright.
- 03: close up of the eye and gills (red gills show freshness).
- 04: on the scale.

**Cuts: steaks and fillets** (seer fish steaks, fillet)
- 01: 4 to 6 steaks laid out in a neat row or circle, top view.
- 02: stack of 3 steaks at 45 degrees to show thickness.
- 03: close up of one cut face (flesh colour, no bones for fillet).
- 04: portion on the scale (500 g or 1 kg).
- Also shoot the whole fish once, so the customer can see where the steaks come from.

**Prawns** (tiger, white; headless or with head)
- 01: a handful on the tray in a loose pile, top view; tails and legs not crushed.
- 02: 5 to 6 prawns laid in a line next to a coin or a ruler to show size.
- 03: close up of the shell and tail.
- 04: on the scale.

**Crab** (mud crab)
- 01: one live or fresh crab, top view, claws tied and tidy.
- 02: underside view (to show it is full) and a side view.
- 03: close up of the shell and claws.
- 04: on the scale.

**Squid and cuttlefish**
- 01: whole cleaned squid in a row, or rings in a pile.
- 02: one whole tube next to a ruler.
- 03: close up of the skin or the rings.
- 04: on the scale.

**Dry fish** (anchovies, shrimp, mackerel)
- 01: a loose heap on the white board, top view, plus the sealed pack beside it.
- 02: sealed pack alone, front facing, label readable.
- 03: close up of a handful in your palm (shows size and dryness).
- 04: pack on the scale (250 g).
- Dry fish is lit best in daylight; no yellow light, it makes the fish look dull.

**Frozen packs** (squid rings, fish fillet)
- 01: sealed pack, front facing, flat on white, label readable.
- 02: pack opened, contents on a white plate (thaw a few pieces first so there is no frost).
- 03: close up of the contents.
- 04: pack on the scale.
- Shoot within a minute or two of taking the pack out so there is no sweat or frost on the plastic.

### Extra shots (not product specific)
- **Boats and landing:** 3 to 5 photos of the boats and the morning catch (for the home page, "The boats" or About page if we keep it).
- **Packing:** the insulated box with ice and the packed order (shows how it is sent).
- **The stall:** one wide shot of the stall and the family.
- These can have a real background. No people's faces without their permission.

## File names
Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes only. No spaces.

`<product-slug>-<two digit number>-<shot>.jpg`

Examples:
- `seer-fish-steaks-01-top.jpg`
- `seer-fish-steaks-02-angle.jpg`
- `seer-fish-steaks-03-close.jpg`
- `seer-fish-steaks-04-scale.jpg`
- `dry-anchovies-02-pack.jpg`
- Extras: `boat-02-morning-catch.jpg`, `stall-01-wide.jpg`

Product slugs must match the product list (see the seed data in Round 1).

## Size and format
- **Format:** JPG (phone default is fine; if the phone saves HEIC, set the camera to "Most Compatible" or convert).
- **Shape:** keep the phone's normal 4:3, or square. The site will crop. Do not crop tightly yourself.
- **Size:** at least 1600 pixels on the long side (any modern phone does this). Under 8 MB per file. Do not shrink or compress them by hand; Cloudinary resizes and compresses automatically.
- **Do not** add text, borders, prices, logos or watermarks to the photos. The site adds the look.
- Keep the originals in a folder `photos/` on your computer or Google Drive (not in the git repo).

## Delivery of photos
Put photos in a shared folder named by product (`seer-fish-steaks/`). In Round 4 the family uploads them in the admin panel; in Round 5 we check each one on a phone. Re-shoot anything blurry, dark, or yellow.
